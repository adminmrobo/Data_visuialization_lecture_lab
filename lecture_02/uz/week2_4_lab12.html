Amaliyot ① · Solishtirish ustunlari — «Qaysi shahar eng ko'p to'laydi?»

P bosqich — avval savol kalitso'zlarini ko'ramiz. «Qaysi shahar» toifa, «eng ko'p» solishtirish → solishtirish → ustunli diagramma.

P bosqich · 4 elementli promptColabda payments.csv bor. Ustunlar: date, hour, city, channel, items, amount, is_new_user. Shaharlar bo'yicha amount summasini vertikal ustunli diagrammada, kattadan kichikka tartiblab chiz. Rang #1E3A8A, fon #FAF7F0, yuqori va o'ng hoshiya chiziqlarini olib tashla. O'zbek tilida javob ber. Kodning har bir qatorini bitta gap bilan tushuntir.
P Step · 4-element promptI have payments.csv in Colab. Columns: date, hour, city, channel, items, amount, is_new_user. Show total amount by city as a vertical bar chart, sorted from high to low. Use color #1E3A8A, background #FAF7F0, remove top and right spines. Answer in English. Explain each line of code in one short sentence.
P 步骤 · 四要素提示词我在Colab里有payments.csv。列名：date, hour, city, channel, items, amount, is_new_user。 请按城市汇总amount，画成柱状图，从高到低排序。 颜色用#1E3A8A，背景用#FAF7F0，去掉上方和右侧的边框线。 请用中文回答。每行代码用一句话解释。

(«kattadan» bajarildimi? rang so'rovga mosmi?) — Bittasi ham farq bo'lsa yana so'raymiz. «Grafik to'g'ri, faqat rang boshqacha. Qayta chiz.» — Inspektor noto'g'ri yetkazilgan buyumni ko'rsatsa yetadi. Xatoni ko'ra olmaslik xavfliroq.

① Iqlis — axlat bormi? (3D · soya · ortiqcha rasm · takroriy legenda → qayta so'raymiz) ② Aniqlik — ustunlarning Y o'qi nolmi? ③ Maqsadlilik — bitta gaplik xulosa bormi?

Bitta gaplik tushuntirish namunasi — «2026-yil mart to'lov summasida Shanxay (2,540 ming von) undan keyingi Pekinning taxminan 2 barobar va Xanchjou Shanxayning yettidan biri xolos». — Bitta gap, bir to'plam raqam, bitta solishtirish.

Amaliyot ② · Tendensiya chizig'i — «To'lov summasi sana bo'yicha qanday o'zgaradi?»

P bosqich — «sana bo'yicha o'zgarish» kalitso'zi → tendensiya → chiziqli grafik. Ikki ogohlantirish — ① 5 shaharni hammasini chizsak 5 chiziq o'raladi → bu safar faqat bitta shahar (Shanxay) ② «sana bo'yicha yig'indi» ekanini aniq so'raymiz.

P+C · Shanxay tendensiyasi — sana bo'yicha yig'indi, birdanigapayments.csv'dan city == 'Shanghai'gina tanlab, har bir date uchun amount yig'indisini hisobla. Keyin shu ma'lumotni chiziqli grafik qilib chizadigan Python kod ber — X o'q date, Y o'q kunlik yig'indi. Chiziq rangi #1E3A8A. Eng baland kunni #B45309 nuqta bilan ajrat. Fon #FAF7F0, yuqori·o'ng hoshiya chiziqlarini olib tashla. O'zbek tilida javob ber. Kodning har bir qatorini bitta gap bilan tushuntir.
P+C · Shanghai trend — daily totals in one goFrom payments.csv, compute the daily total of amount for city == 'Shanghai'. Then give me the Python code to plot a single line chart, X = date, Y = daily total, indigo line. Highlight the highest day with an amber dot (#B45309). Background #FAF7F0, remove top and right spines. Answer in English. Explain each line of code in one short sentence.
P+C · 上海趋势——按日合计一步到位从payments.csv中筛选city == '上海'，按date汇总每天的amount。然后给我Python代码，画一条折线图，X轴是date，Y轴是当天总和，线条用深靛蓝。 最高的那一天用#B45309大点突出。背景#FAF7F0，去掉上方和右侧边框线。 请用中文回答。每行代码用一句话解释。

O bosqich — o'qish: eng yuqori nuqta qachon

Eng yuqori nuqta taxminan mart o'rtasi bo'lishi kerak. Ko'k nuqtalar yig'indagi yagona ambar nuqta — ko'z tabiiy shu yerga boradi va aynan shu bitta gaplik tushuntirishning boshlanish nuqtasi bo'ladi. Bitta gap namunasi — «Shanxayning mart to'lov cho'qqisi 15-mart bo'lib, ish kunlari o'rtachasining taxminan 1,8 barobar». Eng yuqori nuqta bir necha marta ham chiqishi mumkin (masalan: 2-hafta + 4-hafta) — o'sha holatda barchasini ajratish mumkin.

Amaliyot ③ · Taqsimot gistogrammasi + bog'lanish scatter diagrammasi

Taqsimot savoli — «To'lov summasi qayerga to'plangan?» Kalitso'z: to'planish → taqsimot → gistogramma. Ehtiyot — ma'lumotda bo'sh qiymat taxminan 4% bor. Promptga «bo'sh qiymat» bo'lagini qo'ymang, bo'sh emas qiymatlar bilan yig'indini 100%ga yetkazamiz (3-hafta asosiy qismi).

P+C · Summa taqsimoti — bo'sh qiymatsiz, bin 100payments.csv'dan amount bo'sh qatorlarni olib tashlab, amount gistogrammasini chiz. Bin kengligi 100, diapazon 0–900. Eng baland ustunni #B45309 bilan ajrat. Fon #FAF7F0, rang #1E3A8A, yuqori va o'ng hoshiya chiziqlarini olib tashla. O'zbek tilida javob ber. Kodning har bir qatorini bitta gap bilan tushuntir.
P+C · Amount distribution — drop nulls, bin 100From payments.csv, drop rows where amount is null, then plot a histogram of amount with bin width 100 (range 0–900). Highlight the tallest bar with color #B45309. Use background #FAF7F0, color #1E3A8A, remove top and right spines. Answer in English. Explain each line of code in one short sentence.
P+C · 金额分布——排除缺失，bin 100从payments.csv中去掉amount为空的行，画amount的直方图，bin宽度100，范围0–900。最高的柱子用#B45309突出。 背景#FAF7F0，颜色#1E3A8A，去掉上方和右侧边框线。 请用中文回答。每行代码用一句话解释。

Bog'lanish savoli — «Mahsulot ko'p bo'lsa to'lov summasi ham kattami?» O'zgaruvchi 1: items (1–15), o'zgaruvchi 2: amount → bog'lanish → scatter diagramma. items butun son bo'lgani uchun nuqtalar chiqib qoladi, x o'qni ±0.3 atrofida siljitamiz (jitter).

P+C · items × amount — butun son chiqishi → siljitishpayments.csv'dan (amount bo'sh qatorlarni olib tashlab) x = items, y = amount scatter diagrammasini chiz. items butun son, x o'qni biroz siljita qo'y (±0.3 atrofida). Nuqtalar #1E3A8A, shaffoflik 60% atrofida. Fon #FAF7F0, yuqori·o'ng hoshiya chiziqlarini olib tashla, faqat och to'r chiziqlari. O'zbek tilida javob ber. Kodning har bir qatorini bitta gap bilan tushuntir.
P+C · items × amount — integer overlap → jitterFrom payments.csv (drop rows where amount is null), plot a scatter of x = items, y = amount. Apply small horizontal jitter (about ±0.3) because items is integer. Use #1E3A8A dots with about 60% opacity. Background #FAF7F0, no top/right spines, light gridlines only. Answer in English. Explain each line of code in one short sentence.
P+C · items × amount——整数重叠→加抖动从payments.csv中（去掉amount为空的行），画散点图，x=items，y=amount。items是整数，对x加一点水平抖动（约±0.3）。点用#1E3A8A，约60%透明度。 背景#FAF7F0，去掉上方和右侧边框线，只保留浅色网格线。 请用中文回答。每行代码用一句话解释。

O bosqich — anomal nuqtani topish: uzoq turgan bitta nuqta ambar

Boshqa nuqtalardan sezilarli uzoq turgan bitta nuqtani ambar rangida qayta chizamiz. Qayta so'rov namunasi — «Shu scatter diagrammada eng uzoq nuqtani faqat #B45309 qil, hajmi boshqa nuqtalarning 2 barobar. Qolganini o'z joyida qoldir». Nega muhim — anomal nuqta kiritish xatosi ham bo'lishi, haqiqiy hodisa ham bo'lishi mumkin. Hukmni odam beradi. Haqiqiy hodisa bo'lsa, u bitta gapning qahramoniga aylanadi. Namuna — «13 mahsulotga 2,800 ming von to'lov — boshqa nuqtalarning taxminan 2 barobar. Kiritish xatosi kabi, lekin aslida guruh buyurtma bo'lgan».
